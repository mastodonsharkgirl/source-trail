# Source Trail

A static browser-local product with three views: Story Lab, the original operational Evidence Desk, and How It Works. Story Lab uses authored Meadow Atlas fiction, stable selectable ideas, a default no-download recorded real-run walkthrough, manual evidence assessment, optional secondary WebGPU experiment and exact immutable supplied-context inspection. The original six-note retrieval pack and its evaluation remain separately labelled.

The product helps visitors understand the difference between retrieval, context and generation. It does not verify truth, causal attribution, training sources, authorship or plagiarism. Real generation has contradicted supplied world rules; generated prose is visibly marked as needing review.

The additional Policy Lab route at `/policy-lab/` is a local implementation
checkpoint: a fictional policy corpus, shared eligibility/selection, bounded
Python/Ollama runner, assessment/reporting and recorded-only evidence viewer.
