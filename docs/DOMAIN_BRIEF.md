# Source Trail

A static browser-local product with three views: Story Lab, the original operational Evidence Desk, and How It Works. Story Lab uses authored Meadow Atlas fiction, stable selectable ideas, a default no-download recorded real-run walkthrough, manual evidence assessment, optional secondary WebGPU experiment and exact immutable supplied-context inspection. The original six-note retrieval pack and its evaluation remain separately labelled.

The product helps visitors understand the difference between retrieval, context and generation. It does not verify truth, causal attribution, training sources, authorship or plagiarism. Real generation has contradicted supplied world rules; generated prose is visibly marked as needing review.

Astro static output, React interaction islands, one lazy decorative Three.js horizon, a dedicated WebLLM Worker and original Transformers.js/ONNX WASM Worker. No accounts, uploads, analytics, paid inference, remote user-text processing or persistent visitor-content storage. Explicit export is local; public model caches consume disk.

Build with SOURCE_TRAIL_BASE=/tools/source-trail/ for portfolio integration. Integration/deployment belongs to the portfolio owner. Keep the previous source-trail-saran.pages.dev baseline deployment intact. Read MODEL_MANIFEST.json, REVIEW_LOG.md and docs/story-lab/ARCHITECTURE.md before changing claims or runtime boundaries.

## Interface direction — 15 November 2026

### Editorial redesign — 18 November 2026

Story Lab opens with a fictional Meadow Atlas landscape and the rule the model
reversed. The exact supplied passages and saved answer sit side by side on wide
screens and stack in reading order on phones. The manual review follows the
answer. The words experiment, saved request and live generation are further down.

Evidence Desk uses an ivory reading surface, teal masthead and cobalt search
controls. The question, ranked results and original note are separate surfaces.
The initial camera example runs through the existing text-matching code.
Method and source labels explain how to interpret results and open the original.

Meadow Atlas artwork was generated with ImageGen for this redesign. The site
serves one 1280×853 WebP still. The image depicts the fictional setting. Motion is
limited to short reveals and source selection feedback. Reduced-motion
preferences disable those effects, and Story Lab's pause also stops its reveals.

The two useful entry tasks lead the composition: inspect a saved answer against its supplied passages in Story Lab, or search fictional work notes in Evidence Desk. Story Lab retains its deep-teal, paper and cobalt identity; a small original vector trail illustrates passages flowing toward an answer with a visible conflict marker, not a claim that the answer is verified. Evidence Desk opens with a real, explicitly labelled lexical worked example and its source selected. It never downloads a model until the visitor chooses semantic search.

Raised rounded paper panels, restrained shadows and readable contrast distinguish working areas. Controls have quick, small press/hover feedback for fine pointers and static/reduced-motion fallbacks. Compact desktop hero space intentionally leaves the search/result workspace visible early; narrow layouts preserve reading/action order without horizontal overflow. The conflict review remains manual and is explicitly not an automatic support verdict.
